## Extraction caveats

- Four of the per-principle PDFs (`Adapt to changing conditions`, `Integrate development
  with growth`, `be locally attuned`, `be resource efficient`) are **scans with no text
  layer**. Their content is covered by the lecture slides and the checklist.
- `Biomimicry Taxonomy.pdf` is a circular diagram; the extracted text is unordered. Note 02
  reconstructs the branches that matter to us — check against the printed wheel before
  citing it.
- Page numbers given for the Genius of Biome are **PDF pages**, which match the printed
  page numbers in that document.
