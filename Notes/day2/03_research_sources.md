# Where to find natural models — research sources

Source: `Research - Where to look....pdf` (Baumgartner / Musiolik, ZHAW ISD), 10 slides.

## The route

1. Start from a **function** you defined in scoping (their example: "move in liquids").
2. In **AskNature** you find the same pattern as on the Taxonomy wheel — navigate the
   function, not the organism.
3. Take the same function terms to the literature databases.

## Databases named

- www.sciencedirect.com
- link.springer.com
- scholar.google.ch
- Use the operators: `*`, `?`, `AND`, `OR`

The worked example (slides 7–8) shows the useful move: a first search on the *function*
("organism storing electricity") surfaces a **catchphrase** ("microbial fuel cell"), and
the catchphrase becomes the second, much better search. For us the equivalent catchphrases
to hunt for are terms like *radiative sky cooling*, *passive daytime radiative cooling*,
*evaporative facade*, *thermochromic coating*, *biomimetic self-cleaning*.

sit in the photonics literature, not the coatings literature.
