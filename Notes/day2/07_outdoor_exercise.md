# Outdoor exercise — Discovering

Source: `Outdoor exercise Discovering.docx`.

## Objectives

Practice mindful observation and sharpened perception · develop curiosity and openness ·
notice patterns, strategies and connections in living systems · capture ideas by sketching,
writing, sharing.

## Steps

1. **Sit spot and sensory awareness** (10 min, alone) — three slow breaths, focus on sound.
   Notice what you see, hear, feel, smell, without judging. Look close, then far: "pretend
   you are a tiny insect, then a bird in the sky". Write or sketch what catches attention.
2. **Pattern hunt** (10 min, team) — look for spirals, branches, symmetry, waves, cracks,
   tessellations. Collect at least three examples as sketches, notes or photos.
3. **Metaphor play** (5 min, team) — each person picks one object and completes
   "This [object] is like [something unrelated] because…". Their example: *"This leaf is
   like a solar panel because it collects energy from the sun."*
4. **Connection web** (10 min, team) — pick one local organism, brainstorm everything it
   connects to, draw the web.
5. **Reflection** (10 min) — one surprising thing, one metaphor or pattern enjoyed, one new
   question. Closing question: *"How might noticing deeply help us become better designers
   in general and for our challenge?"*

## How to make this count for the report

The exercise is the **naturalist lens** from the lecture, and it is also the cheapest source
of figures. Two things are worth doing deliberately while outside:

- Run the **pattern hunt on a hot sealed surface** rather than on a lawn: a south-facing
  wall, a stone kerb, a gravel strip, a tree trunk in full sun. Which organisms are living
  there, and what are they doing about the heat? That is the *operating condition lens* and
  the *"find the truly challenged"* prompt from slide 18, executed in the field.
- **Photograph what you find.** Section 3.1 needs figures, and the report currently contains
  no photographs at all. A field photo with a caption that names the mechanism is worth more
  than a stock image.
