## The three things to act on first

1. **The matrix takes 3–5 functions, not thirteen.** Slide 22 gives the filter, and the note
   applies it to F0–F12 and produces a defensible five-row matrix.
2. **The evaluation table needs three rows we do not have**: feasibility, biomimetic transfer,
   and evidence / uncertainty.
3. **Scale is a named failure mode.** Four of our models are micro-scale; each one needs a
   sentence on whether the effect survives being made square metres large.
