Source: `Team formation process and world cafe setting.pptx`.

## Timeline

```
DAY 1  speed networking + challenge wall  →  preliminary teams of 3–4
DAY 2  world café / project marketplace   →  final team of 4, registered in Moodle
DAYS 2–5  biomimicry process, group work and coaching
AFTER  complete the report using the Moodle template  →  one report per team, 70 %
```

**Final team membership is fixed at the end of Day 2** — after that, no changes.

## The world café format — what we have to deliver

- Each challenge has a table / meeting point.
- Choose **1 presenter and 1 timekeeper**; everyone else is audience and moves around.
- **A 5-minute pitch**, laptop allowed, covering:
  1. challenge and scoping
  2. system of interest
  3. initial project direction
  4. **open questions**
- Then a **10-minute discussion round with visitors — "Get feedback!"**
- **4 rotations**, presenter may change between rounds.

## Why this matters for the report

The rubric requires, under *Concept Evaluation & Iteration*:

> "Use **peer review (in the world café presentation or before)**, instructor feedback and/or
> AI as a critical reviewer to identify weak assumptions and open questions."

So the world café **is** the peer-review evidence for Section 3.3. That only works if someone
writes the feedback down during the rounds. Concretely, for each of the four rotations
capture: who raised what objection, and whether we accepted it.

The pitch content maps one-to-one onto our scoping deck, so no separate slides are needed —
but the **"open questions"** item is the one we would otherwise skip, and it is the item most
likely to generate usable feedback. Good candidates from where we stand: whether F9 may be a
threshold rather than a continuous response; whether the water path survives a Swiss winter;
whether the concept should target roof or façade.

## Team roles (slide 4)

taker** — with the questions "which role would you take?" and "how does this role impact
teamwork?". Relevant to the title-page contribution table, which is still empty in our
