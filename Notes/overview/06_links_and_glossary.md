# Links and glossary

## Glossary terms the graders use

Source: `Biomimicry glossary.pdf` (Biomimicry 3.8 Resource Handbook; only page 1 has a text
layer). The definitions worth using precisely:

- **Biologize** — "to take a human need or function and rephrase it so that an answer may be
  found in biology, e.g. *'How can I make the fabric red?'* becomes *'How does nature create
  colour?'*"
- **Biologist at the Design Table (BaDT)** — a biologist adept at combing through nature's
  solutions and translating them into design ideas. (The "BaDT brainstorm" boxes in the
  Genius of Biome are exactly this.)
- **Biology to Design** — the approach that *starts* from natural models and proceeds through
  abstraction, brainstorming, emulation and evaluation. The opposite direction —
  challenge-first — is what we are doing.
- **Abiotic / biotic** — abiotic factors include **sunlight, temperature, wind patterns and
  precipitation**: precisely our operating conditions.
- **Analogous** — "similar in function and/or appearance but not in origin or development".
- **3.85 billion years** — "the research and development period of life".
