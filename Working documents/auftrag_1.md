   3. Anpassung an bestehenden gebäuden

2. Systems View
   1. Super-system wäre stadt/district
   2. System of interest haben wir vorerst mehrere wie Hitzeabbau sowie preventive hitze verhindern aber nicht auf ein Gebiet wie facade eingeschränkt für das erste
   3. Parallele Systeme: Kanalisation, strassen netz, stromnetz, Bevölkerung/Öv/Verkehr, etc.
   4. Subsystems: Oberfläche, Isolierung, Material, arbeiter welches dies aufbauen müssen, maintainer, wetter standhaltung
3. Functions
   1. Main function: Regulate heat in city
   2. Reflect short-wave radiation & Prevent absorption of solar energy in the first place, Emit long-wave radiation & Release heat to the sky, in particular in the atmospheric window, Transfer heat to ambient air & Convective dissipation at the surface, Store thermal energy temporarily & Buffer and shift the daily peak, Shade a surface & Reduce the irradiated area, e.g.\ through surface geometry
   3. Guide heat or store it for winter for heat/energy generation
4. KPI's
   1. Urban Heat index
   2. albedo/ Solar reflection quotient
   3. Peak surface temperature
   4. Net radiative cooling power at 02:00, clear sky; and night air temperature effect
   5. Sustainabilty costs
   6. Building costs
5. Design Question
   1. How do we prevent heat build up in Urban areas
   2. How could we use the energy/heat build up positivly while lowering temperature
   3. How might we get the heat that Zurich's existing buildings store during the day back out of the city before the next morning, without using energy?
   4. How can we prevent heat waves causing heat islands
   5. How might we turn the heat that accumulates in a City into a useful resource, instead of simply trying to reflect/discard it city
