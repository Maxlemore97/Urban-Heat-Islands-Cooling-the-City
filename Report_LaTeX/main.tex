\section{Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
  \item[Short-wave radiation] The radiation the sun delivers, roughly
    \qtyrange{300}{2500}{\nano\metre} (ultraviolet, visible and near-infrared).
    It is what a surface can \emph{reflect}; the fraction reflected is the solar
    reflectance $\alpha_\mathrm{sol}$ of criterion~2. Function F2 acts on it.
  \item[Long-wave radiation] The thermal radiation a surface at ordinary
    temperatures emits by itself, roughly \qtyrange{4}{50}{\micro\metre}. It is
    what a surface can \emph{give away}, including at night and to a clear sky,
    and it is governed by the emittance $\varepsilon$ rather than by
    $\alpha_\mathrm{sol}$. Function F4 acts on it. The two are separate
    properties: a surface can be bright in the short wave and still a poor
    emitter in the long wave, which is why criterion~2 and criterion~5 are
    reported apart.
  \item[Atmospheric window] The band near \qtyrange{8}{13}{\micro\metre} in
    which a clear sky is largely transparent, so long-wave radiation emitted
    there escapes to space rather than being returned. It is what makes
    night-time radiative cooling below air temperature possible.
  \item[Albedo] The share of incoming short-wave radiation a surface reflects.
    The city classes $\leq 0.30$ as low and $\geq 0.80$ as high
    \parencite[128]{stadtzuerich2020}.
  \item[PET] Physiologically equivalent temperature: the temperature a person
    would experience given air temperature, radiation, humidity and wind. It is
    the metric the client uses to judge a measure at street level, and it is a
    different quantity from surface temperature.
  \item[UHI] Urban heat island: the persistent temperature difference between a
    city and its rural surroundings, caused by urban surface properties and
    geometry \parencite{oke1982}. In Zurich it reaches about \qty{7}{\kelvin} at
    night \parencite{stadtzuerich_merkblatt}.
  \item[Solution-neutral] A statement of \emph{what} must happen that names no
    means of achieving it. Design questions and functions in this report are
    written this way so the biology can propose the means.
\end{description}
