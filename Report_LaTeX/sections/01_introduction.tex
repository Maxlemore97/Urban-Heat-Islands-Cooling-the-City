\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Background \& Relevance}

The Urban Heat Island (UHI) effect represents a growing environmental and public
health challenge, characterized by significantly elevated temperatures in urban
environments relative to surrounding rural areas~\cite{yang2016}. This effect is
worsened by climate change and a high concentration of heat absorbing
infrastructure, like cement and asphalt. Conventional mitigation strategies rely
heavily on air conditioning, which creates a counterproductive cycle
(Section~\ref{sec:problem}).

\subsection{Design Challenge \& Project Contribution}

The design challenge lies in developing an efficient, scalable cooling system
capable of mitigating the urban heat island effect without relying on high energy
consumption. The goal is to analyze the different heat dissipation methods found in
nature, specifically in animals, to find a solution which conforms with the given
challenges.
