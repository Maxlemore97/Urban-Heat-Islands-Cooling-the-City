\subsection{Background \& Relevance}
\label{sec:background}

The Urban Heat Island (UHI) effect represents a growing environmental and public health
challenge, characterized by significantly elevated temperatures in urban environments
relative to surrounding rural areas \cite{yang2016}. This effect is worsened by climate
change and a high concentration of heat absorbing infrastructure, like cement and asphalt.
Conventional mitigation strategies rely heavily on air conditioning, which creates a
counterproductive cycle. It increases external temperatures through heat rejection and
elevates the energy consumption, often causing higher greenhouse gas emissions.

To address this problem, the report investigates nature inspired passive cooling
mechanisms, also known as biomimicry.

\subsection{Design Challenge \& Project Contribution}
\label{sec:contribution}

The design challenge lies in developing an efficient, scalable cooling system capable of
mitigating the urban heat island effect without relying on high energy consumption.

The goal is to analyze the different heat dissipation methods found in nature,
specifically in animals, to find a solution which conforms with the given challenges.
